Scans for Commercial Real Estate

Best for: Brokers, owners, landlords, tenants, managers of office, retail and industrial space

Download the one-page PDF Get an instant quote

A 3D tour lets a prospective tenant or buyer walk a space from anywhere, and a floor plan from the scan gives everyone the same layout to talk about.

What you get

  • A 3D tour of vacant space, built-out suites or whole buildings.
  • Dollhouse and floor-plan views.
  • Exterior 360° shots and optional photos.
  • Tags: notes you can attach inside the tour to point out features.
  • On request: point-cloud and CAD files for planners.

How to use your tour

  • Link it from your listing, brochure and email campaigns.
  • Send it to out-of-town decision makers before a site visit.
  • Share it with space planners and architects.

Don’t worry about

  • Learning the software. Tours open from a link.
  • Being on site the whole time, as long as we have access.

How to prepare

  • Confirm access for the whole building: lobby, suites, stairwells and any areas to include.
  • Unlock doors so rooms connect, and turn on all lights.
  • Remove confidential papers and cover or turn off screens that show private information.
  • Occupied suites: coordinate a time with the tenant and ask people to stay out of view.
  • Large or open spaces take more scan positions and time. Tell us the size.

Good to know

  • Leased area in commercial buildings is commonly calculated with a measurement standard. For office buildings that is ANSI/BOMA Z65.1-2024, and industrial space has its own standard, ANSI/BOMA Z65.2-2025. A floor plan from a scan is not a certified BOMA measurement.
  • A scan is a visual record from one day. It is not an inspection, an appraisal or a survey.
  • Use scan dimensions for planning, and verify critical measurements on site.

Document Your Home Before You Need To

Best for: Homeowners, renters, anyone who wants a baseline record of a home

Download the one-page PDF Get an instant quote

After a fire, flood or storm, it helps to show what you had and what condition it was in. A scan gives you a dated walk-through of every room, plus a floor plan.

What you get

  • A 3D tour of each room, as it looked on scan day.
  • A measured floor plan.
  • A link you can store with your insurance and home records.

Add what a scan cannot show

  • A written list of belongings: brand, model, serial number, purchase date and price.
  • Close-up photos of valuables and receipts for high-value items.
  • A copy of your policy, kept somewhere safe and waterproof.

Don’t worry about

  • Staging or cleaning. Tidy is enough.
  • Being there during the scan, as long as we can get in.

How to prepare

  • Open closets, cabinets and any storage you want recorded.
  • Put away valuables and personal papers you would rather not appear in the scan.
  • Turn on all lights.
  • Secure pets, and ask people to stay out of the room being scanned.
  • Decide whether to include the garage, basement, attic and outbuildings.
  • Save the tour link in your email and with your policy documents.

Good to know

  • The Pennsylvania Insurance Department recommends photos or video of your belongings to prove ownership, keeping receipts, and keeping a copy of your policy in a safe, waterproof place.
  • A scan shows what is visible. Items inside drawers, boxes or closed cabinets are not recorded unless opened.
  • Whether an insurer accepts a scan is the insurer’s decision. Ask what they need.

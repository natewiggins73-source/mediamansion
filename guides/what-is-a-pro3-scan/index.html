What Is a Matterport Pro3 Scan?

Best for: Anyone new to 3D tours

Download the one-page PDF Get an instant quote

A Matterport Pro3 is a camera and laser scanner on a tripod. It captures a space from many positions, and software joins those captures into a 3D model you can walk through in a web browser.

How it works

  • We set the Pro3 on a tripod and capture from position to position through the space.
  • Each position takes under 20 seconds.
  • The Pro3 uses LiDAR (laser distance measuring) plus a camera, so the model has both shape and color.
  • Matterport’s software aligns everything into one model, and you receive a link.

What you get

  • A walk-through tour.
  • Dollhouse and floor-plan views.
  • Measurements, when measurement mode is on.
  • Tags: notes and labels attached to spots in the tour.
  • Optional files for design, claims and planning software.

Accuracy in plain language

  • Matterport rates the Pro3 at about ±20 mm at 10 m.
  • That is good for planning and for most design work.
  • For anything cut or ordered to fit, verify critical measurements on site.

What can affect a scan

  • Matterport says bright sunlight, shiny floors, fog, dust and rain can add noise or gaps. The Pro3 is designed for variable lighting, including outdoors in direct sunlight.
  • Objects and people should stay put during a scan. Anything that moves can leave artifacts.

What a scan is not

  • An inspection, appraisal, survey, structural assessment or engineered drawing.

Good to know

  • You do not need to be there, buy anything or install an app. We bring the equipment, and the tour opens in a browser.
  • A scan shows what is in view on scan day. If the space changes, ask about a rescan.

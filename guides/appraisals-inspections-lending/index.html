Scans for Appraisals, Inspections & Lending

Best for: Appraisers, home inspectors, lenders, agents and sellers

Download the one-page PDF Get an instant quote

A scan is a dated, visual and dimensional record of what is visible. It is a useful reference, but it does not replace the professional doing the appraisal or inspection.

What a scan can do

  • Give a shareable, dated walk-through of the property on scan day.
  • Provide a floor plan that shows the layout.
  • Help a reviewer who cannot visit in person get oriented.
  • Serve as a before-and-after record for repairs or changes.

Don’t worry about

  • Us replacing your professional. We do not appraise, inspect or assess damage.
  • Learning software. A tour is a link.

What a scan is not

  • An appraisal, home inspection or damage assessment.
  • An ANSI Z765 measurement. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac require appraisers to follow ANSI Z765-2021 when measuring, and a scan floor plan is not an ANSI measurement report.
  • A survey, structural assessment or engineered drawing.

Before you book

  • Ask the appraiser, inspector or lender what they accept.
  • Tell us who will receive the link.

Good to know

  • Floor-plan sizes from a scan are approximate. Do not substitute them for a measurement the appraiser or lender requires.
  • Rules belong to the appraiser, inspector, lender and your MLS. We follow what they ask for.
  • Matterport rates the Pro3 at about ±20 mm at 10 m. That is a measure of the camera, not a standard for square footage.

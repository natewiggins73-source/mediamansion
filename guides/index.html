Quick answers

The questions we hear most

Do I need to be there?

Not the whole time, as long as we can get in.

Do viewers need an app?

No. A tour opens in any web browser.

How long does it take?

Each scan position takes under 20 seconds. Total time depends on the size and layout. Standard tours are delivered within 24–48 hours.

What will it cost?

Get an instant estimate with the instant quote.

Where do you work?

Greater Philadelphia, the Main Line, and Bucks, Montgomery, Chester and Delaware Counties, plus New Jersey, Delaware and Maryland. Travel is included within 50 miles of ZIP 19144.

Good to know. A scan is a visual record from scan day. It is not an inspection, appraisal, survey or engineered drawing. Listing sites, MLSs and insurers set their own rules, and each guide tells you what to check.

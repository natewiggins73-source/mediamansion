Scans for Businesses & Storefronts

Best for: Shops, salons, studios, offices, clinics, gyms, showrooms

Download the one-page PDF Get an instant quote

A 3D tour shows customers the inside of your location before they visit. For businesses, a tour can also be published to Google Street View so the interior can appear on Google Maps.

What you get

  • A 3D tour you can link from your website, Google Business Profile and social profiles.
  • Optional interior photos.
  • Google Street View publishing for businesses.
  • Blurring of faces, screens or signage on request.

Don’t worry about

  • Closing your business. Pick a quiet hour and we work around you.
  • The Google process. We publish from Matterport and handle the setup.
  • Every small detail. Faces, screens and signs can be blurred afterwards.

How to prepare

  • Choose a quiet time. Staff and customers in view during a scan can appear in it.
  • Set up displays, menus and seating the way customers should see them.
  • Put away cash, keys and paperwork, and turn off or cover screens that show private information.
  • Turn on all lights, including display lighting.
  • Tell us about private areas to leave out.
  • Give us a point of contact and entry instructions.

Good to know

  • Google’s policy does not allow imagery of the interior of private residences, so Street View publishing is for business locations only.
  • Google decides when and how published imagery appears. Publishing does not guarantee a particular placement or timing.
  • If your layout changes or you renovate, the tour will no longer match. Ask about a rescan.

Before Your Scan Appointment

Best for: Everyone: agents, owners, managers, contractors

Download the one-page PDF Get an instant quote

A scan goes smoothly when we can get in, the lights are on and the space is clear. This page covers what helps and what you can safely skip.

What to expect

  • A confirmation email within 12 hours of your booking request. Your booking is final once you receive it.
  • Scan day: we capture position by position. Total time depends on size and layout.
  • Your tour within 24–48 hours of the appointment. Technical files such as CAD or BIM take longer, and the timing is listed when you order.
  • An invoice within 24 hours of the appointment. Payment comes before final files are released.

Don’t worry about

  • Moving or staging furniture. Show the space as it is normally seen.
  • A deep clean. Tidy is enough.
  • Being there the whole time, as long as we can get in.
  • Equipment, power, apps or software. We bring everything.
  • Rain on scan day for interiors. Exterior work can be rescheduled with no fee.

Do this before we arrive

  • Confirm access: choose lockbox, door code, or who will meet us when you book, and make sure the code works.
  • Unlock and open interior doors so rooms connect.
  • Turn on every light, replace dead bulbs and open the blinds.
  • Tidy floors and surfaces, put away valuables and personal papers, and hide loose cords.
  • Secure pets, and ask people to stay out of the room being scanned.
  • For exterior shots, move cars off the driveway and put bins out of sight.
  • Tell us about special features to capture, or areas to leave out.

Good to know

  • Please give 24 hours’ notice to change or cancel. A fee can apply inside that window, or if we arrive at a property we cannot access or that is not ready. See the booking terms for the details.
  • Please do not rearrange furniture or items once we start. Matterport notes that moving objects during a scan can cause artifacts.
  • Travel is included within 50 miles of ZIP 19144. Beyond that, a per-mile fee applies and is calculated when you book.

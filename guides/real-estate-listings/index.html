Scans for Real Estate Listings

Best for: Agents, sellers, brokerages

Download the one-page PDF Get an instant quote

A 3D tour lets buyers walk a home online before they book a showing, and gives out-of-town buyers a real sense of layout and scale. It works best alongside professional photos.

What you get

  • A Matterport 3D tour with dollhouse and floor-plan views.
  • Exterior 360° shots.
  • Optional: measured floor plan, listing photos, walkthrough reel, property website, social media kit and virtual staging.
  • One link that opens in any web browser. Viewers need no app.

Don’t worry about

  • Learning the technology. You get a link.
  • Choosing photos or a tour. Most listings use both.
  • Being there during the scan, as long as we can get in.

How to prepare

  • Scan after the home is cleaned and staged, the way you want buyers to see it. The scan shows whatever is in view.
  • Book the scan and photos for the same visit.
  • Vacant home: turn utilities on and make sure every light works.
  • Turn on all lights and open the blinds.
  • Tell us which spaces to include: basement, garage, outbuildings.
  • Move cars and bins before exterior shots.
  • Secure pets and keep people out of the room being scanned.

How to use your tour

  • Paste the link into your MLS virtual tour field. Rules differ by MLS, and some require an unbranded link. Tell us and we will set it up.
  • Add it to your listing website, emails and social posts.
  • Print a QR code that links to the tour on flyers and signs.

Good to know

  • Listing sites decide what they display. Zillow removed Matterport tours from its listings on October 20, 2025, during a dispute with Matterport’s owner. Check whether your tour displays there before you rely on it.
  • MLS rules differ on tour links and edited photos. If you use virtual staging or edits, disclose them wherever your MLS requires.
  • Floor-plan sizes from a scan are approximate. Check your MLS’s rules before you list square footage taken from one.

Sharing & Publishing Your Tour

Best for: Agents, owners, managers, hosts, business owners

Download the one-page PDF Get an instant quote

Your tour is a link. You can put it on a lot of places, but each platform sets its own rules. This page covers where it works, and where to check first.

Easy places to use it

  • Your own website: link it or embed it.
  • Email, text messages and email signatures.
  • Social posts and bios.
  • Print: a QR code on flyers, signs and brochures.
  • Your Google Business Profile, for a business location.

Need it private?

  • Ask us for a password-protected tour.
  • Send it only to the people who need it.

Check first

  • MLS: rules differ. Some require an unbranded link with no agent or broker contact details. Ask us and we will provide one.
  • Zillow: removed Matterport tours from its listings on October 20, 2025, during a dispute with Matterport’s owner. Check whether your tour displays before you rely on it.
  • Vrbo: no longer supports virtual tours as a listing feature, though you can add a link to your description.
  • Other rental and listing sites: policies change. Check before you plan around them.
  • Google Street View: for business locations only. Google’s policy does not allow interior imagery of private residences.

Good to know

  • We can provide a branded link, an unbranded link or both. Tell us what your MLS or platform requires when you book.
  • Google decides when and how published Street View imagery appears.
  • Platform rules change. This guide was last reviewed in November 2026. If something looks out of date, email hello@mediamansion.com.

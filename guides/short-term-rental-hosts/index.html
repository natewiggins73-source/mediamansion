Scans for Short-Term Rental Hosts

Best for: Airbnb and Vrbo hosts, vacation-rental owners, furnished-rental managers

Download the one-page PDF Get an instant quote

A 3D tour shows guests the layout, the sleeping arrangements and how rooms connect before they book. It works on your own website, social pages and direct-booking links.

What you get

  • A 3D tour with a floor-plan view.
  • Exterior 360° shots.
  • Optional photos, floor plan and walkthrough reel.
  • One link that opens in any browser.

Where to use it

  • Your own booking website.
  • Social posts and email to past guests.
  • A QR code in your welcome guide or on a flyer.
  • Your listing description, where the platform allows links.

Don’t worry about

  • Technical setup. You get a link.
  • Scheduling around guests. We book a gap between stays.

How to prepare

  • Book a day with no guests in the property.
  • Set the space the way guests will find it: beds made, towels folded, surfaces clear.
  • Put away personal items, papers and valuables.
  • Replace dead bulbs, turn on every light and open the blinds.
  • Include the spaces guests can use: patio, deck, hot tub area, garage.
  • Move cars and bins before exterior shots.

Good to know

  • Platforms change what they accept. Vrbo no longer supports virtual tours as a listing feature, though you can place a link in your description. Check your platform’s current rules before you plan around it.
  • Show the space honestly. A tour that matches what guests find protects your reviews.
  • Local rules and platform rules for rentals still apply. A tour does not change them.

Scans for Insurance & Restoration

Best for: Homeowners, landlords, adjusters, restoration contractors

Download the one-page PDF Get an instant quote

A scan creates a dated walk-through record of a property’s condition, plus a floor plan. It can support before-and-after comparisons, claim conversations and restoration estimates.

What you get

  • A 3D tour showing every room as it stood on scan day.
  • A measured floor plan.
  • On request: a TruePlan sketch file (.SKX) for Xactimate.
  • A Property Intelligence Report: an automated, room-by-room measurement report.

Don’t worry about

  • Being there during the scan, as long as we can get in.
  • Learning any software. Tours open in a browser.
  • Choosing file formats. Tell us who needs the files and we will match them.

How to prepare

  • Scan a healthy property for a baseline, or scan damage before repairs begin when it is safe to enter.
  • Follow your insurer’s and adjuster’s instructions. Do not delay safety work for a scan.
  • For damage records, do not clean up first unless safety requires it.
  • Open the closets and cabinets you want recorded.
  • Tell us who receives the files (adjuster, contractor, carrier) and which format they need.
  • Keep your tour link and files with your policy records.

Good to know

  • The Pennsylvania Insurance Department advises photos or video of damage before cleanup or repairs, temporary repairs to prevent more damage, and no permanent repairs until the insurer inspects and approves. Keep every receipt.
  • A scan documents what is visible. It is not an inspection, an appraisal or a damage assessment.
  • Whether a carrier accepts a particular file is the carrier’s decision. Ask your adjuster what they need before you book.

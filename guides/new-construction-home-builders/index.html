Scans for New Construction & Home Builders

Best for: Builders, developers, sales teams, model homes

Download the one-page PDF Get an instant quote

Scan a model home so buyers can tour it from anywhere. Scan homes under construction to keep dated records of each stage, then scan the finished home for handoff.

What you get

  • A 3D tour of a model or finished home, with dollhouse and floor-plan views.
  • Dated scans of work in progress, so you can look back at any stage.
  • Exterior 360° shots.
  • Optional: measured floor plan, photos, video and a property website.
  • On request: point-cloud, CAD or BIM files for your design team.

When to scan

  • Model home: once it is finished and furnished.
  • Open-wall stage: before insulation and drywall, to record framing, plumbing and wiring.
  • Final: when the home is complete.

Don’t worry about

  • A tidy site. Work in progress is fine.
  • Final plans. The scan records what is actually there.

How to prepare

  • Tell us the stage of the build when you book.
  • Clear walkways and move loose materials where you can.
  • Pause work in the area being scanned. People who move through during a scan can appear in it.
  • Make sure doors are unlocked and power is on where you want lights lit.
  • For communities, send us your lot or model list and preferred days. We schedule multi-property work by email.
  • Tell us early which file type your team needs.

Good to know

  • Matterport rates the Pro3 at about ±20 mm at 10 m. Use scan dimensions for planning, and verify critical measurements on site before ordering materials or cutting.
  • Matterport says bright sunlight, shiny floors, fog, dust or rain can add noise or gaps to a scan. Heavy dust or rain can mean we reschedule or rescan.
  • A scan is not an inspection, a survey or an engineered drawing.

Which Files Do I Need?

Best for: Anyone asked to “send a file” by a designer, contractor, adjuster or architect

Download the one-page PDF Get an instant quote

Most people only need the tour link. If someone else needs more, such as an adjuster, architect or contractor, this page matches the file to the job. When unsure, tell us who is receiving it and what software they use.

FileWhat it isOften requested by
Tour linkThe 3D walk-through, with dollhouse and floor-plan views. Opens in any browser.Everyone
Floor planA picture of the layout with room sizes. Sizes are approximate.Agents, owners, designers
Property Intelligence ReportAn automated, room-by-room measurement report.Agents, owners, adjusters
MatterPakA bundle with a textured 3D model (OBJ/MTL), a point cloud (.xyz) and floor and ceiling plan images.Designers, 3D and CAD users
E57 point cloudA standard point-cloud file for point-cloud and BIM software.Architects, engineers, scanning teams
CAD floor plan2D drawings as DWG files, with DXF versions.Architects, drafters, contractors
BIM modelA 3D building model as a Revit (RVT) or IFC file, at LOD 200.Architects, engineers, builders
TruePlan sketchA sketch file (.SKX) that opens in Xactimate.Adjusters, restoration contractors

Good to know

  • Tell us who is receiving the files and which software they use, and we will match them. You do not need to know the format names.
  • Turnaround for technical files is listed when you order. As a guide: 1–2 business days for MatterPak and E57, 3–5 for CAD, and 2–7 for BIM, longer for large or complex properties.
  • Whether a carrier, architect or contractor accepts a particular file is up to them. Ask what they need before you order.

Scans for Renovation & Remodel Planning

Best for: Homeowners, contractors, designers, architects

Download the one-page PDF Get an instant quote

A scan records a room as it is, so designers and contractors can plan without repeat site visits. Repeat scans also give you a before-and-after record.

What you get

  • A 3D tour you can measure from, when measurement mode is turned on for your model.
  • A measured floor plan.
  • On request, files for design software: MatterPak, E57 point cloud, CAD and BIM.
  • Turnaround is listed with each file when you order.

Don’t worry about

  • Cleaning up a work in progress. Capturing what is there is the point.
  • Having final plans. The scan is a starting point.
  • File formats. Tell us the software or who is receiving the files and we will match it.

When to scan and how to prepare

  • Before demolition, to record existing conditions.
  • At the open-wall stage, to document framing, plumbing and wiring before walls close.
  • When finished, for a final record.
  • Clear walkways and move small items where practical.
  • Tell your designer or contractor early which file type they need (CAD, BIM or point cloud).

Good to know

  • Matterport rates the Pro3 at about ±20 mm at 10 m. Use scan dimensions for planning and design, and verify critical measurements on site before ordering materials or cutting.
  • A scan is not a structural assessment, a survey or an engineered drawing.
  • Furniture and belongings in view appear in the scan. For the cleanest layout, scan the room empty or with items moved out.

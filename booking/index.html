What happens next

  1. We'll email you within 12 hours to confirm your date and time and ask about anything else we need — including any custom-quote items. Your booking isn't final until you receive that confirmation.
  2. We capture the property at the time you selected in the scheduler.
  3. Standard listing media is delivered within 48 hours of the shoot. Technical files (MatterPak, CAD, BIM, and similar) follow the business-day turnaround listed with each item.
  4. Payment is collected on the day of the shoot or shortly after — always before your final files are released. Your invoice is sent within 24 hours of the completed appointment.

Before we arrive

Please make sure the property is accessible, lights are working (where applicable), and rooms are ready to photograph or scan. Pets should be secured, and any special areas you'd like captured should be noted in advance. Exterior and aerial work may be rescheduled if weather makes a safe or quality capture impractical — we'll coordinate a new time with you.

Media usage

Your delivered media includes the rights you need for property marketing — MLS, social media, websites, print materials, and brokerage promotion — subject to Media Mansion's service terms. Media Mansion retains copyright except as needed for MLS submission.

Cancellations & rescheduling

Need to change plans? Give us at least 24 hours' notice. A $50 fee applies to cancellations or reschedules inside that window, or if the property isn't accessible or ready to photograph when we arrive.

Questions or changes? Email hello@mediamansion.com or call (215) 779-7235.

Terms & Conditions

Cancellation & rescheduling

Please notify us at least 24 hours before your scheduled appointment to cancel or reschedule. A $50 fee applies to appointments canceled or rescheduled inside this window.

Property access & readiness

If we arrive and the property cannot be accessed or is not ready to be photographed, a fee of 25% of the booked order total (minimum $50) applies before we can reschedule. A travel fee may also apply if we are given an incorrect address.

Payment

Nothing is charged when you submit this form. Payment is collected on the day of the appointment or shortly after, and always before final deliverables are released. Your invoice is sent within 24 hours of the completed appointment. Applicable sales tax and any custom-quote items are reflected on that invoice.

Travel

Travel is included for properties within about 90 miles (roughly 2 hours) of our Philadelphia base. Beyond that, a travel fee of $1.25 per one-way mile is calculated automatically from the property address and appears on your order summary and invoice.

Weather

Exterior and aerial services may be rescheduled when weather conditions make safe operation or quality capture impractical. We will coordinate a replacement date with you, and no rescheduling fee applies in that case.

Custom quotes

Properties over 8,000 sq ft, Full Walkthrough Videos for those properties, and video edits over 3 minutes are quoted individually. Selecting one of these is a request for a quote, not a charge — we will contact you with pricing before any work is scheduled.

Delivery & turnaround

Standard listing media is delivered within 48 hours of the end of the appointment. Technical files (MatterPak, CAD, BIM, and similar) follow the business-day turnaround listed with each item on the order form.

Photos captured

You receive the exact number of photos ordered. Please use the fields on this form to note any specific features or areas you'd like photographed. We typically skip closets, unfinished areas, storage rooms, and garage interiors unless otherwise specified.

Licensing & MLS

Media Mansion retains copyright of all photos, videos, floor plans, and digital media produced, except as needed for submission to the MLS. In exchange for payment, you are granted a license to use the delivered media to market and promote the property and your real estate business.

Media usage

Your delivered media includes the rights you need for property marketing — including MLS, social media, websites, print materials, and brokerage promotion — subject to these service terms. Media may not be resold, sublicensed, or used by third parties to create new media without express written consent from Media Mansion.

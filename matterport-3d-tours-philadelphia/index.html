Before the scan

What is a Matterport 3D tour?

A Matterport tour is an interactive 3D model of a space. A Matterport Pro3 camera captures panoramic photos and depth measurements from positions throughout the property, and Matterport’s software joins them into a walkable tour with a dollhouse view and a floor-plan view. Visitors explore it from a link in any browser, with nothing to install.

How is a 3D tour different from listing photos?

Photos are selected, edited stills that show a property at its best in a feed or MLS gallery. A 3D tour lets people move through the whole space themselves, see how rooms connect and get a feel for scale. Most agents use both, which is why packages such as the Premier Combo include photos, a 3D tour and a floor plan.

How long does the scan take?

The Pro3 captures each scan position in under 20 seconds, but total time depends on how many positions your property needs. Larger homes, more rooms and tighter layouts take longer. Tell us the size and layout when you book and we will give you a realistic estimate.

Do I need to be there?

Not necessarily. When you book, you choose how we get in: a lockbox, a door or keypad code, or someone meeting us at the property. If someone is there during the scan, we ask that people and pets stay out of the room being captured.

How should I prepare?

Tidy up, turn on every light, open interior doors and blinds, put pets away and move cars off the driveway if you want exterior shots. Our free prep guide is a one-page checklist that also lists what you can skip.

Do I need to move furniture or stage the home?

No. We scan the space as it is, so show it the way you want buyers or renters to see it. Virtual staging and clutter removal are available for listing photos (not for the 3D tour) when you book.

Can you scan mirrors, windows and shiny surfaces?

We scan them, but mirrors, large glass and very dark or glossy surfaces can look odd in any 3D model because the depth sensor does not read them reliably. If a large mirror or glass wall matters to you, mention it when you book.

Can you scan outdoors?

Yes. The Pro3 works outdoors, including in bright sun, which is how we capture the exterior 360° shots that come with every tour. Interiors go ahead in almost any weather. Heavy rain, strong wind or poor light can delay exterior capture, and if that happens we will reschedule with no fee.

What if I need to reschedule, or the property is not ready?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice. A $50 fee applies to cancellations or reschedules inside that window, and if we arrive and the property cannot be accessed or is not ready to photograph. Full details are in the booking terms.

Deliverables & your tour

What do I get after the scan?

A shareable tour link and exterior 360° shots, delivered within 24 to 48 hours. You can add a measured floor plan ($40) and, if your designer, architect or adjuster needs them, technical files such as MatterPak, E57 point cloud, CAD, BIM or Xactimate TruePlan sketch files. Still frames pulled from the scan are available on request.

How do I share or embed my tour?

You receive a link that opens on desktop and mobile with no app. Add it to your MLS virtual tour field, listing website, emails or social posts, or embed it on a website. How a tour appears on a particular MLS or listing site depends on that site’s rules, so check where you plan to post it. See the sharing guide for where tours work and what to check first.

How long does my tour stay online?

Every tour is hosted for 90 days at no extra cost. After that, keeping it online is $20 per month.

Who owns the tour and the files?

Media Mansion keeps the copyright. Once you have paid, you receive a license to use the delivered media to market and promote that property, including MLS, social media, websites and print. It cannot be resold or passed to third parties without written permission. See the terms for details.

How accurate are the measurements?

Matterport rates the Pro3’s raw depth data at about ±20 mm at 10 m. Real-world accuracy depends on the space and how it is scanned, so treat tour measurements as a planning and documentation tool, not a replacement for a licensed survey or engineered drawings. Verify critical dimensions on site before ordering materials. For a plain-language explanation, read What Is a Pro3 Scan?

Do I need special software to view or use the files?

No. Tours open in a browser. Technical files such as E57, CAD and BIM are for professional design software like AutoCAD or Revit, which your architect or contractor will already use. Our file guide shows which file suits which job.

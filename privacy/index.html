Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026  ·  Last Updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you visit mediamansion.com (the “Site”).

By using the Site, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, such as when you submit a booking request or send an email. The instant quote tool only asks for your property size and ZIP code, to estimate your price and travel. A booking request may include:

  • Name
  • Email address and phone number
  • Property address and access details you choose to enter (for example, how we will get into the property)
  • Any other information you choose to share in your order notes or message

We do not knowingly collect any sensitive personal information or data from individuals under the age of 13.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit for the following purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries or messages you send through the Site
  • To schedule, carry out and invoice the work you book
  • To communicate with you about potential projects or bookings
  • To deliver your finished media
  • To maintain basic internal records

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Service Providers & Third-Party Content

We use a few outside services to run the Site and booking tools. Booking requests are sent to us by email through EmailJS. Address and ZIP lookups and travel-distance estimates use OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim and OSRM), and the booking map uses Leaflet with OpenStreetMap map tiles. Appointments are scheduled through Google Calendar’s appointment scheduler.

The Site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, portfolio videos from Vimeo and 3D tours from Matterport. When these load, those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details, under their own privacy policies.

4. Cookies & Analytics

This Site may use cookies or basic analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to collect non-personal information about your visit, such as:

  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Pages visited
  • Duration of visit
  • General geographic region (not precise location)

This information is used only to improve the functionality and performance of the Site.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings if you prefer not to have this data collected.

5. Data Retention

We retain submitted information for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry or maintain a record of legitimate business interest. You can request deletion at any time (see Section 7).

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.

7. Your Rights

If you are located in the EU, UK, California, or other regions with data protection laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct or delete your information
  • Withdraw your consent to processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: hello@mediamansion.com

8. Third-Party Links

This Site may include links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites and encourage you to read their policies separately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes to the Site or legal requirements. The updated version will always be posted here with the most recent date.

10. Contact

For any questions or requests related to this Privacy Policy, please contact: hello@mediamansion.com
